G·E·S·T·A·L·T — The Brand Code

G Galant — Noble bearing, effortless authority in every room
E Elegant — Refined restraint that signals knowledge, not effort
S Sporty — Ease and movement without sacrificing precision
T Traditional — Timeless construction, tailoring as foundation
A Artistic — Style as self-expression and identity architecture
L Luminary — The client becomes the most noticed person in the room
T Tough — Durable style that holds up across decades and contexts

Digital strategy

Instagram · LinkedIn · website · the enigmatic brand question

◈
The core tension — and how to resolve it
Joseph wants the Beyoncé-mode enigmatic presence (no posts, rare drops) but also wants to attract young creative professionals who discover brands digitally. The resolution: LinkedIn is where the substance lives. Instagram is where the mystique lives. Website is where bookings happen. These are three different channels with three different jobs.
Instagram — The Enigmatic Channel
The no-posts strategy — what it actually means
No posts until opening. A single, impeccable bio. gestaltatelier.com in the link. An enigmatic profile image — architectural, not personal. When the first post eventually drops, it should feel like an event. Both partners approve every Instagram post before it publishes, always.
Both
Stories at the launch event — the only pre-opening content
Consider allowing Stories (not feed posts) from the launch event. Stories disappear — they feel exclusive and in-the-room. The attendees' own stories and shares become the social proof. Gestalt Atelier's own account stays clean.
B
Bio copy
Maximum 3 lines. Suggested: "Image Consultancy. Financial District, New York. Sessions from $2,500 — gestaltatelier.com." Nothing else. No hashtags. No call to action. No emoji.
A writes

Joseph's personal profile — credentials and myth-making
A fully built LinkedIn profile with a long-form About section. The Business Insider credential prominently featured. A headline that says something like: "Image Consultant · Financial District, NYC · Gestalt Atelier." Not "founder" or "CEO" — those words are everywhere.
